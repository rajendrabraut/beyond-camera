# Beyond Camera 1.0.1.0

This build is for testing before a wider rollout.

## Downloads

- **BeyondCamera-1.0.1.0-release.apk** is the signed Android upgrade for Beyond Camera. It keeps the existing app ID and signing certificate. Captures and processing work offline.
- **BeyondCamera-1.0.1.0-diagnostics.apk** is the separately installed debug build. It keeps the app ID and certificate of the corrected Diagnostics APK from 1.0.0.12 and 1.0.0.13. Pair it from **Settings → Capture diagnostics** using the [private dashboard](https://beyond-camera-diagnostics.plummaze.chatgpt.site/).
- **BeyondCamera-1.0.1.0-release.aab** is the Android App Bundle for store submission. Install the APK directly when testing a phone.

## Capture and gallery

- A fresh launch always opens in Photo. Photo mode can select a sharper pre-shutter frame when it matches the scene. The camera requests optical stabilization where supported.
- Buffered photos write directly into Android MediaStore, with EXIF orientation. Photos appear in `Pictures/BeyondCamera` and videos in `Movies/BeyondCamera`. The original appears first. Optional photo enhancement updates the same gallery entry.
- **Settings → Change save folder** selects the album folder under Pictures and Movies. New photos use `BYND_IMG_yyyyMMddHHmmssSS.jpg` with 16 UTC timestamp digits and unique names during a burst. Existing gallery photos still appear in the viewer.
- On Android 10 and later, Time-lapse writes into a pending gallery video while recording, avoiding a full file copy at stop when direct writing is supported. Video and Clip log the time to start and finalize. Night keeps its first frame available while the extra frames are processed.
- Diagnostics reports frame acquisition or JPEG encoding, worker wait, and gallery-write times separately. The app sends an original JPEG after gallery save. Video sends recording events and duration. The signed Android release excludes the upload code and internet permission.

## What testing found

- On the Xiaomi M2006C3MI, 1.0.0.12 reported buffered photos at 960 × 720 and hardware stills at 2448 × 3264. The longest observed photo took about 4.39 seconds to reach the gallery. The phone later reported successful video saves of 9.2 and 18.5 seconds.
- In the 1.0.0.13 emulator test, gallery writes accounted for 482–975 ms in the four buffered reports. A separate rapid-tap test saved four of six shots when the camera fell back to hardware still capture. Repeat testing on physical phones is needed to qualify rapid tap behavior, handheld photo quality, and Samsung installation.
- Android packages target Android 8.0 or later and include ARM and x86 native libraries. Camera resolutions and available controls vary by device.

## iOS

The repository includes an iOS 16+ app built with SwiftUI and AVFoundation. It captures photos and videos and saves to Apple Photos through PhotoKit by default. The folder button selects an alternate Files destination. The GitHub workflow builds a simulator app and compiles an unsigned device build. Installable distribution requires Apple signing and provisioning plus camera testing on a physical device. The iOS app currently covers photo and video capture. Android-only modes and hosted diagnostics are outside this iOS build.
